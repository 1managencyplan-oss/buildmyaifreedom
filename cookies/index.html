Cookie Policy

The short version: this website sets no cookies at all.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

buildmyaifreedom.com sets no cookies. No analytics, no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no advertising or retargeting tags, no session recording, and nothing stored in your browser's local storage. That is also why you don't see a cookie consent banner on this site — there is nothing to consent to.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store, so it can recognise you on your next visit. Cookies are what make advertising retargeting and most analytics possible. We don't use any.

What this site does load

Although we set no cookies, our pages do make requests to a few outside services. Here is the full list:

ServiceWhenWhat it means for you
Google FontsEvery page loadTypefaces are fetched from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser information in order to send the font files. Google states that it does not set cookies for these requests.
Google Apps ScriptOnly when you submit the enquiry formYour submission is sent to our private spreadsheet.
WhatsAppOnly when you click a WhatsApp buttonYou leave our site and WhatsApp's own cookie and privacy practices take over.
GitHub PagesEvery page loadOur host serves the page and, like any web server, records the request.

If this ever changes

If we add analytics or an advertising pixel in future — which is normal for a marketing business — we will update this page first, and where the law requires it we'll ask for your consent before anything non-essential loads.

Controlling cookies yourself

Even though we don't set any, you can manage or block cookies for every site from your browser's settings, usually under "Privacy" or "Site settings". Blocking cookies will not affect how this site works, because it doesn't rely on them.
