Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who else can see it, and how to get it deleted.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Who we are

Build My AI Freedom ("we", "us", "our") operates buildmyaifreedom.com and provides AI-powered performance marketing services from India. For any privacy question or request, email support@buildmyaifreedom.com.

What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and the enquiries we receive through it. If you become a client, the work we do for you is also governed by the separate agreement we sign with you — where that agreement says something different about confidentiality or data handling, the agreement applies to that work.

What we collect

Information you give us

Our enquiry form asks for four things: your name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, and your reason for connecting. Nothing on this site asks for payment card details, identity documents or passwords. If a page claiming to be ours ever asks you for those, it is not us — please tell us.

Information recorded alongside your enquiry

When you submit the form, these are stored with it so we know where the enquiry came from:

  • date and time of submission
  • the page address you submitted from, and the page that referred you
  • your browser's local time and timezone

If you message us on WhatsApp

Clicking a WhatsApp button on this site opens a chat with us. Your phone number, profile name and messages then become visible to us, and WhatsApp handles that conversation under Meta's own privacy policy, not ours.

What we do not collect

This site sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising or retargeting pixels, no session recording, and stores nothing in your browser. There are no user accounts and no logins. See our Cookie Policy.

How we use it

  • to reply to you and arrange your free audit
  • to prepare recommendations and proposals
  • to deliver and support our services if you become a client
  • to keep ordinary business records of enquiries and agreements

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not upload enquiry details into advertising platforms to build audiences.

Who else can see it

We use a small number of service providers. Each one only receives what it needs:

Beyond these, we share your information only if the law requires it, or if we need to establish or defend a legal claim.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we're in contact with you, and afterwards as part of our business records. You can ask us to delete your details at any time and we will remove them from our records, unless we are required to keep something for legal, tax or accounting reasons.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information about you we hold, and who we've shared it with
  • correct anything inaccurate or incomplete
  • erase your information
  • withdraw a consent you previously gave
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you are unable to

Email support@buildmyaifreedom.com and we will act on your request as quickly as we reasonably can, within the timelines the law requires.

Keeping it safe

Enquiries land in a private spreadsheet that only the founders' Google account can open, and this site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive personal information — identity numbers, bank details, passwords — through our enquiry form or WhatsApp.

Information handled outside India

Google and GitHub operate globally, so your information may be stored or processed on servers outside India under those providers' own safeguards.

Children

Our services are sold to businesses and this site is not directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this changes we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If we ever start using analytics or advertising pixels, we'll update this page and the Cookie Policy before turning them on.

Contact and grievances

For any privacy question, request or complaint, email support@buildmyaifreedom.com.

This policy describes the site exactly as it stands on 2 October 2026: four form fields, no cookies, no analytics, four service providers. We'd rather keep it specific and true than long and vague.
